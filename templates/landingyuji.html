Why Carry exists

Burnout is rarely one big thing. It's everything piling up — without anyone noticing — until it's too late.

Uni students already juggle assignments, part-time jobs, social commitments, errands, and whatever's left for sleep. Nobody says yes to burnout. They say yes to one more thing, eleven times, before anyone — including them — notices what the week actually costs.
