You don’t decide to burn out. You just say yes eleven times.

Seven days. Seven reasonable yeses. Carry shows you the weight of the week before you agree to it — then gives you somewhere to put it down.

The model

An average lets three calm categories hide one that’s on fire.

Time 130, everything else 40 averages to a comfortable 58 — and that comfortable 58 is exactly the problem. So Carry weights the peak.

item_cost = hours x (0.6 + 0.2 x effort) category[c] = sum(item_cost) / budget[c] CAPACITY = 100 x ( 0.6 x max(cat) + 0.4 x mean(cat) ) budgets TIME 40 MENTAL 25 PHYSICAL 20 SOCIAL 18 ERRANDS 10

Cost of Yes is not a second engine. It is that same function called twice, once with the new thing appended. Nothing to be wrong, nothing to fail on stage.

Prototype. Everything is local to this browser — no account, no server, and nothing you type or say leaves the tab.
